The Honda Pilot

The Honda Pilot is a midsize SUV manufactured by Honda. It is designed for families and drivers who need plenty of passenger space, comfort, and versatility. With its bold styling, powerful V6 engine, and advanced technology, the Pilot is suitable for everyday driving, long road trips, and outdoor adventures. It offers three rows of seating and combines the practicality of a family SUV with the performance needed for a variety of driving conditions. The exterior of the Honda Pilot features a large front grille, modern LED headlights, and a muscular body design. Its wide stance, sculpted body panels, and stylish alloy wheels give it a strong and confident appearance. Depending on the trim level, the Pilot can feature roof rails, a power tailgate, a panoramic-style sunroof, and additional rugged styling details. The TrailSport trim has a more adventurous design, with features intended to improve its ability to handle rougher terrain.

The interior of the Honda Pilot is spacious and designed for family comfort. Depending on the configuration, it can seat up to eight passengers across three rows. The cabin features a touchscreen infotainment system, smartphone connectivity, and a digital instrument display on equipped models. Higher trims may include leather-trimmed seats, heated and ventilated front seats, a heated steering wheel, and a premium Bose sound system. The second and third rows can be folded to create additional cargo space for luggage, groceries, and outdoor equipment

The Honda Pilot is powered by a 3.5-liter V6 gasoline engine that produces approximately 285 horsepower in recent models. It uses a 10-speed automatic transmission to deliver smooth acceleration and comfortable highway driving. Depending on the trim, the Pilot is available with front-wheel drive or Honda's i-VTM4 all-wheel-drive system. The TrailSport trim adds off-road-oriented features, including increased ground clearance and specialized suspension tuning, making it better suited for certain outdoor adventures.
