The Roadster

The Tesla Roadster is designed to be an exciting electric sports car with a futuristic look inside and out. Its interior is expected to focus on a sporty, minimalist design, with premium materials and a driver-focused layout. The next-generation Roadster has been announced by Tesla, but its final production interior may differ from the designs shown so far.The Roadster is expected to feature a modern cockpit with a clean dashboard and advanced technology. Its low seating position is intended to create a sports-car driving experience, while the cabin is designed to complement its sleek exterior. Tesla has also presented the Roadster with a removable glass roof, which could provide an open-air feeling when conditions allow.

The most unusual feature is the Roadster's proposed flying capability. Tesla's Rocket version uses SpaceX-inspired cold-gas thrusters positioned around the vehicle to produce upward and forward movement, as well as rotation in the air. Tesla demonstrated a prototype performing airborne stunts in October 2026, but the production vehicle's real-world capabilities have yet to be fully verified.
