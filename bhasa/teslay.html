Tesla Full Self-Driving (FSD) is an advanced driver assistance system designed to help drivers with many driving tasks. Depending on the vehicle, software version, and region, it can help steer, accelerate, brake, follow routes, change lanes, and respond to traffic lights and stop signs. It uses cameras and artificial intelligence to understand the road and help the car navigate different driving situations. FSD makes driving more convenient, but it does not mean the car is fully autonomous. The driver must remain attentive, monitor the road, and be ready to take control whenever necessary. Tesla continues to improve its driving technology through software updates, with the goal of making driving safer and more advanced.

The Tesla Model Y is an electric car, so it does not need gas. Instead, you charge it by plugging it into a charger. You can charge it at home overnight or use a Tesla Supercharger when you are on a long trip. Superchargers are much faster than regular home outlets and can add a lot of driving range in about 15 minutes. The exact charging time depends on the charger and the battery.The Tesla Model Y has electric motors that help it move. Some versions have more power than others, and the faster versions can go from 0 to 60 mph in around 3.3 seconds. It also has quick acceleration, which means it can pick up speed very fast. Since it runs on electricity instead of gasoline, it does not have a regular gas engine.
