The BMW 4 Series Gran Coupe

The BMW 4 Series Gran Coupe is a sporty luxury car manufactured by BMW. It combines the sleek appearance of a coupe with the practicality of a four-door car. Its sloping roofline, modern styling, and powerful engine options give it a distinctive appearance. The Gran Coupe is designed for drivers who want a stylish vehicle that offers sporty performance, everyday comfort, and useful cargo space. The exterior of the BMW 4 Series Gran Coupe features a bold front grille, sharp LED headlights, and sculpted body lines. Its low roofline and wide stance create a sporty appearance, while the frameless side windows add a premium touch. Depending on the trim, the car can feature an M Sport body kit, larger alloy wheels, a rear diffuser, and other sporty design elements. At the rear, its sleek taillights and sloping hatchback design give it a modern and elegant look.

The interior of the BMW 4 Series Gran Coupe combines luxury with advanced technology. It features BMW's curved display, which brings together the digital instrument cluster and central infotainment touchscreen. The cabin includes comfortable seats, a multifunction steering wheel, and high-quality materials. Depending on the configuration, available features may include leather upholstery, ambient lighting, heated seats, and a premium sound system. Its rear liftgate opens widely, making it easier to load luggage, shopping bags, and other cargo.

The BMW 4 Series Gran Coupe offers a range of engines depending on the model and trim. The 430i Gran Coupe uses a 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine producing approximately 255 horsepower. The M440i Gran Coupe features a 3.0-liter turbocharged inline-six engine with mild-hybrid technology, producing approximately 386 horsepower. Depending on the version, the car is available with rear-wheel drive or BMW's xDrive all-wheel-drive system. Its responsive acceleration and balanced handling make it enjoyable to drive on both city streets and highways.
