-- About us --

Hello, I am Raj Khatiwora, a 12-year-old student at Bhasa Academy. I developed this website to provide prospective buyers with additional information before purchasing a vehicle. I included all of the cars that i could with hard work so if I do not have the car you are looking for, I am deeply sorry. I would like to acknowledge my parents, whose guidance and support have been instrumental; I am deeply grateful for their role as mentors in my life. This project also stems from my interest in automobiles, which I explore through automotive edits on my YouTube channel. Thank you for visiting this site; your interest is greatly appreciated.
